# Copywriting — OFF by default

**The skill does NOT write copy.** The heading is `[title]` and the body is `[content]` — she fills
both herself (her merge pipeline / her editor). Your job is the *frame*, not the words.

Only if she **explicitly asks** you to draft copy (e.g. "viết luôn nội dung giúp"), then:
- One clear-benefit headline (≤ ~8 words, no full stop), 1–2 sentence intro, ONE action-verb CTA.
- Scannable benefits (short `✓` lines), tone per industry (warm for beauty, confident for tech…).
- Vietnamese = full diacritics, NFC (see SKILL hard rule 8). Match the brief's language.

Otherwise: leave `[title]` + `[content]` as tokens and move on.
