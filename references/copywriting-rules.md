# Copywriting / messaging rules (what the email SAYS)

Design makes it look good; copy makes it convert. These govern the words. Pair with the
**typography format** rules in `design-guidelines.md` (no full stop on headlines, bullets < 12
words, etc.) — those are about form; these are about message.

## Headline
- Lead with the ONE main benefit or the occasion — clear, not clever. Short (≤ ~8 words).
- For a promo, put the offer in it ("Bắt đầu hành trình IELTS — giảm 20%").

## Body
- **Intro: 1–2 sentences max.** Who you are + the value, then move to the offer/CTA.
- Typical shape: headline → short intro → 2–4 benefits → offer → CTA. Not a wall of text.
- **Benefits**: short, scannable, ONE idea each (a `✓` list). Concrete, not vague
  ("Chấm chữa Writing bằng AI", not "công nghệ tiên tiến").

## Offer & CTA
- **ONE primary CTA per email.** Don't cram multiple offers/asks — it dilutes the click.
- CTA label = an action verb: *Book now · Claim 20% off · View collection · Start free trial ·
  Nhận ưu đãi · Đặt lịch ngay*. (No full stop — see `design-guidelines.md`.)
- State the offer plainly, once, prominently (the offer box).

## Tone per industry (match the trade + the brief's language)
| Industry | Tone |
|---|---|
| Nail / spa / beauty | warm, inviting, a little indulgent |
| Restaurant / food | appetising, friendly |
| Real estate / luxury / decorators | refined, professional, understated |
| Fitness / gym | energetic, motivating |
| Education / IELTS | clear, encouraging, professional |
| Tech / SaaS | concise, confident, benefit-led |

Match the **language** of the brief (Vietnamese ↔ English) and the audience.

## Don'ts
- No jargon / filler ("leading provider of…"). No ALL-CAPS shouting (except tiny eyebrows).
- Don't over-promise; keep claims credible.
